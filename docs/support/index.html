How do my pages get to my other devices?

Through iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account on each device and leave Sync with iCloud on in Bite's Settings. A page changed on two devices at once keeps the changes from both.

How do I format text?

Type Markdown at the start of a line: # and a space for a heading, - and a space for a list, 1. and a space for a numbered list, [] for a to-do, > and a space for a quote. Wrap words in ** for bold or * for italic. Or select text and pick a style from the bar above the keyboard.

How do I clear a page?

Choose Clear Text from the … menu. Changed your mind? Undo brings it back.

Where else can I use Bite?

  • Widgets on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and the desktop show a page or its to-dos, which you can tick off there.
  • Siri and Shortcuts open a page and add to-dos.
  • Spotlight finds the lines on your pages.
  • On a Mac, Bite lives in the menu bar. Set a keyboard shortcut for it in Settings.
  • On Apple Watch, read your pages and tick off to-dos.
